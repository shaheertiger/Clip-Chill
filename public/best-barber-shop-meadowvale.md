## What Makes a Barbershop Worth Your Time

### Consistency, Visit After Visit

A shop's best day isn't the benchmark. The benchmark is the same clean fade and crisp neckline every time you sit down — no matter which barber is free. If quality depends entirely on one person being in, you're gambling, not getting groomed.

### A Proper Consultation

Skilled barbers ask before they cut. How do you style it? How long since the last cut? What didn't work last time? That short conversation is the difference between a cut that looks great walking out and one that grows out cleanly instead of falling apart after ten days.

### Real Skill With Every Hair Type

Straight, wavy, curly, and coily hair all behave differently. A good barber reads the texture and adjusts — blending a fade so it keeps its shape as it grows rather than blurring into nothing. That's experience, not something learned in a few weeks.

## What to Check Before You Book

**1. Reviews with real detail.** Look past the star count. Reviews that name the barber, describe the style, and mention how the cut held up are the signal of genuine repeat customers.

**2. A dedicated men's barbershop.** Shops built around fades, tapers, and beard work are trained for exactly what men's hair needs — and it shows in the finish.

**3. Clear posted pricing.** If you have to ask the cost, expect surprises. Transparent pricing means no add-ons sprung on you after the cut.

**4. Walk-in flexibility.** A good shop handles both walk-ins and appointments, so you're covered whether you planned ahead or not.

**5. Cleanliness.** Sanitized tools, wiped stations, fresh capes. Basic — and a shop that skips it here is skipping it everywhere.
