## Cuts That Suit Thinning and Grey Hair

As hair thins, the goal usually shifts from adding style to making what's there look fuller and tidier. A few approaches tend to work well:

- **Shorter on the sides, a little longer on top.** Keeping the sides neat makes the top look fuller by contrast.
- **A classic taper rather than a skin fade.** A taper looks clean, grows out gracefully, and doesn't need a visit every two weeks.
- **A short, even crop.** When hair is thinning across the top, cutting it shorter and more uniform reduces the contrast between thinner and thicker areas.
- **A neat side part.** A timeless choice that works well with fine hair and needs very little styling.
- **Avoid long strands combed over.** It almost always draws more attention to thinning, not less.

Tell your barber where your hair is thinnest and how you usually comb it. They can tailor the cut to your growth pattern rather than giving you a one-size-fits-all short back and sides.

## Grey Hair Has Its Own Texture

Grey and white hair often behaves differently from the hair it replaced. It's typically coarser and more wiry, and it can stick out in places your hair never used to.

A few things help:

- **A slightly shorter cut** can keep wiry grey hair from looking unruly.
- **A good conditioner** softens coarse grey hair and makes it easier to comb.
- **A light cream rather than gel** gives control without a stiff or shiny look.

Many men find a well-kept grey cut looks sharper than they expected. If you're unsure, ask your barber what would suit your hair now rather than repeating what you've always had.

## Ears, Nose and Eyebrows: Just Ask

With age, hair tends to grow more noticeably on the ears and in the nose, and eyebrows can become longer and more unruly. A neat cut can be undone by a few long eyebrow hairs.

If any of this bothers you, **ask your barber about it during your appointment**. They can tell you what they're able to tidy up and how. Separately, our menu does include **face wax ($10–$20)** and **face threading ($15)** if you'd like something more thorough.
