## Why Proximity Isn't Everything

The closest barbershop to your house is rarely the best one. That's not a knock on convenience — it's just reality. A great cut depends on the skill, consistency, and attention of the barber, and none of those things are guaranteed by a short walk from the village.

Plenty of Streetsville residents have learned this the hard way: they book the nearest shop a few times, get an uneven fade or a neckline that's a little off, and quietly start looking elsewhere. ## What Separates a Great Barbershop From a Decent One

### Consistency Across Every Visit

A good shop on its best day isn't the goal. The goal is the same clean fade, the same crisp neckline, every single time — regardless of which day you come in or which chair is open. If a shop can only deliver when one specific barber is working, you're not getting a service, you're getting a lottery ticket.

### A Real Consultation

The best barbers ask before they cut. How do you usually style it? How long since your last visit? What didn't you like last time? That two-minute conversation is the difference between a cut that looks good walking out the door and one that still looks right three weeks later when it's growing out.

### Genuine Expertise With Texture

There's a difference between someone who cuts hair and someone who understands it. Straight, wavy, curly, and coily hair all behave differently, and a skilled barber adjusts the approach to match — blending a fade so it holds its shape as it grows rather than turning into a shapeless blob after ten days.

## What to Check Before You Book

**1. Detailed reviews, not just a star count.** A wall of "great place!" reviews tells you nothing. Look for reviews that name the barber, describe the style, and mention whether the cut held up. That detail is the fingerprint of real, repeat customers.

**2. A dedicated men's barbershop.** Shops built specifically around fades, tapers, and beard work are trained for exactly what men's hair needs. That focus shows up in the result.

**3. Posted, transparent pricing.** If you have to ask what it costs, that's a red flag. Clear pricing means no surprise add-ons after the clippers go down.

**4. Walk-in availability.** Life doesn't always allow a week of advance planning. A good shop handles both walk-ins and bookings so you're covered either way.

**5. Cleanliness.** Stations wiped between clients, tools sanitized, fresh capes. It's basic — and if a shop cuts corners here, it's cutting them everywhere.
