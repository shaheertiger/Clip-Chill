# Buzz Cut Guide: Lengths, Styles and How to Keep It Sharp

**A buzz cut is a short haircut done almost entirely with clippers, usually at one guard length all over, from a #0 (about 1 mm) up to a #4 (about 13 mm).** It's the easiest cut to live with and the least forgiving to get wrong: with no length on top, the guard you choose and the sharpness of the edges are the whole haircut.

Here's how we help clients at Clip & Chill pick a buzz they'll still like in two weeks.

## Buzz cut lengths chart

| Guard | Length | How it looks |
|---|---|---|
| #0 (no guard) | ~1 mm | Almost all scalp; the shortest true buzz |
| #1 | 3 mm | Very short, scalp clearly visible |
| #2 | 6 mm | Short and clean, a little scalp shows |
| #3 | 10 mm | The classic buzz, scalp mostly hidden |
| #4 | 13 mm | Soft and full; the safest first buzz |
| #5–#6 | 16–19 mm | Longest that still reads as a buzz |
| #7–#8 | 22–25 mm | Closer to a short crop |

For how guards combine in fades, see our [clipper guard sizes chart](/clipper-guard-sizes-explained).

## Which buzz cut length should you get?

- **First time going short: #4.** It hides the scalp and any bumps or scars you didn't know you had. Go down a number next visit if you want.
- **Most people: #3.** Short, even and low-maintenance, with enough length to look like hair.
- **Dark, thick or coarse hair: #2.** It still looks full at 6 mm.
- **Fine, light or thinning hair: #2 or #3 with faded sides.** Shorter sides make the top look denser. Our [thinning hair guide](/haircuts-for-thinning-hair-men) explains why.
- **Hot summer, zero styling: #1.** Expect visible scalp and plan on sunscreen.

## What's the shortest buzz cut?

A **#0, the clipper blade with no guard**, is the shortest buzz at roughly 1 mm. Anything shorter is a shaved head, done with a foil shaver or a razor. If that's where you're heading, read our [head shave guide](/head-and-beard-shave-guide).

## Buzz cut styles

### Induction cut

One very short length all over, usually a #0 or #1. Clean and minimal, and it shows the shape of your head, so it suits people with an even head shape.

### Butch cut

A slightly longer single length, typically a #3 or #4. The most wearable buzz for most face shapes.

### Fade buzz

The top stays at one guard, for example a #2 or #3, and the sides fade down to the skin. It's the most requested buzz in our shop because the contrast gives the cut shape. It's the same blend as our [skin fade](/skin-fade-mississauga), just with a short top.

### Burr cut

Between an induction and a butch cut, often a #1 or #2, finished with a squared, sharp hairline.

### Crew cut

Not quite a buzz: a little more length at the front, tapering shorter toward the crown and sides. Pick it if you want the ease of a buzz with a bit of shape to push forward or up.

## Does a buzz cut suit your face shape?

- **Oval or square:** any length and any style.
- **Round:** a fade buzz. Tight sides make the face look longer.
- **Long:** keep it at a #3 or #4 and avoid a high fade, which adds more height.
- **Receding hairline:** a short buzz makes the hairline look deliberate instead of thinning.

Not sure about yours? Our [haircuts by face shape guide](/haircuts-by-face-shape-men) goes deeper.

## What makes a buzz cut look professional

1. **A lineup.** A crisp edge at the forehead and temples is the biggest difference between a barbershop buzz and one done in a bathroom.
2. **A clean neckline.** Blocked, rounded or tapered: pick one and keep it even. See [neckline styles](/neckline-blocked-rounded-tapered).
3. **Even length on the crown.** Hair grows in a swirl there, and a home clipper pass usually leaves it patchy.
4. **A beard that connects.** If you have one, a [beard lineup](/beard-lineup-mississauga) and a faded cheek join the two together.

## How often should you get a buzz cut redone?

Every **two to three weeks** for a fade buzz or a lined-up hairline. A single-length #3 or #4 without a fade can stretch to four weeks before it looks grown out.

## Looking after a buzz cut

- **Sunscreen on your scalp** from May to September.
- **Moisturise your scalp in winter.** Short hair leaves it exposed to dry indoor heat. Our [dandruff and dry scalp guide](/dandruff-dry-scalp-men) covers the flakes.
- **Wash with a gentle shampoo.** There's no hair to protect, so a harsh shampoo goes straight to your skin.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What number is a standard buzz cut?**
Most people mean a #3 (10 mm) all over. A #2 is shorter with some scalp showing, and a #4 is softer and fuller.

**Is a #2 or #3 better for a buzz cut?**
A #3 is better for a first buzz or lighter hair. A #2 works well on dark, thick hair, where it still looks full.

**Can I get a buzz cut with a fade?**
Yes. Keep the top at one guard and fade the sides to the skin. It's the most popular way to wear a buzz at Clip & Chill.
