# The Clip & Chill Package: What's Included and Is It Worth $110?

Most visits to a barbershop are quick and practical: in, cut, out. The Clip & Chill Package is the other kind. It's about an hour and a half that covers nearly everything on our menu, from the haircut and beard to a hot towel shave, a facial and a shoulder massage.

At $110, it's the most expensive thing we offer. So it's fair to ask what you actually get, how it compares with booking the services separately, and who it makes sense for.

## What's Included

The package bundles nine services into one appointment:

- **Haircut**, including skin and taper fades
- **Beard trim**, with shaping and cheek and neck lines
- **Hot towel shave**, with straight-razor work
- **Skin care**
- **Facial**
- **Shoulder massage**
- **Face wax**
- **Face threading**
- **Hair wash and styling**

Everything is done in one chair, in one sitting, with no need to book separate appointments or move between services.

## How the 1.5 Hours Usually Runs

The exact order is up to your barber and depends on what you're having done, but a typical package follows a logical flow from structure to finish:

1. **Consultation.** A few minutes on what you want from the cut and beard, and anything you'd like to skip or focus on.
2. **Haircut.** The main structural work, done first so everything else is shaped around it.
3. **Beard trim.** Length and shape, blended into the haircut at the sideburns.
4. **Hot towel shave.** Hot towels to soften the hair and skin, then straight-razor work. If you're keeping a beard, this is where the cheek and neck lines get their sharpest finish.
5. **Face wax and threading.** Tidying stray hair on the ears, nose, cheeks and brows.
6. **Facial and skin care.** Cleansing and treatment for the skin after the razor work.
7. **Hair wash and styling.** Washing out clippings and styling the finished cut.
8. **Shoulder massage.** The finish.

Plan for about **an hour and a half** in the chair, and leave a little buffer on either side rather than booking something immediately after.

## How to Prepare

A few small things make the appointment better:

- **Book ahead, especially on weekends.** An hour and a half is a big block in a barber's day, and weekend slots go first. Weekday daytime is the easiest time to find one.
- **Grow the beard a little.** If you want a beard trim and shape, give it a week or two since your last trim so there's something to work with.
- **Skip shaving at home for a couple of days** before the appointment if you're having the hot towel shave. A bit of growth gives a better, smoother result.
- **Come with clean, product-free skin** if you can, so the facial and skin care work on your skin rather than on product.
- **Bring a reference photo** for the haircut if you're trying something new.
- **Say what you'd like to skip.** If you don't want threading or would rather focus on the shave, tell your barber at the start.
