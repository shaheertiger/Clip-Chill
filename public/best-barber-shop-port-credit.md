## What Separates a Great Barbershop From a Decent One

### Consistency Every Single Visit

The goal isn't a good cut on a good day. It's the same clean fade and sharp neckline every time, regardless of which barber is in the chair. If a shop only delivers when one specific person is working, you're rolling the dice each visit.

### A Real Consultation

The best barbers ask first. How do you style it day to day? How long since your last cut? What didn't you like last time? Those couple of minutes are the difference between a cut that looks good walking out and one that still looks right two or three weeks later.

### Genuine Skill Across Hair Types

Straight, wavy, curly, and coily hair each behave differently. A skilled barber adapts to the texture — blending a fade so it keeps its shape as it grows rather than blurring out after a week and a half. That's earned through years of real reps.

## What to Check Before You Book

**1. Detailed reviews.** Skip the star count and read for specifics — barber names, the style, whether the cut lasted. Detail is the mark of real, repeat customers.

**2. A dedicated men's barbershop.** Shops built around fades, tapers, and beard work are trained for exactly what men's hair needs.

**3. Transparent pricing.** If you have to ask the price, that's a warning sign. Clear pricing means no surprise add-ons.

**4. Walk-in availability.** A good shop handles both walk-ins and bookings, so you're covered however your week shakes out.

**5. Cleanliness.** Sanitized tools, wiped stations, fresh capes. If a shop cuts corners here, it cuts them everywhere.
